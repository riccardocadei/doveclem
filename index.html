Mixer shows every track at once — the tall dot is the playhead, the bar on the right mutes. Pattern programs the selected track; Track sets its level. Speed and Chop are what turn a voice memo into an instrument. Pick Lead and the lane becomes eight rows of the scale — tap one to write a note. Giro, next to Key, moves the bass and the chord stab through four bars and decides whether that scale is minor, major or the tarantella's. Suono swaps the stab and the lead between italo saws and accordion reeds. Grooves cut to a particular pack appear only when that pack is loaded.
